About this page
Printful and any cut-and-sew partner route orders automatically once they're connected as a Shopify fulfillment service — Shopify dispatches the order and receives tracking back directly, with no custom integration code in the middle. This page reads that connection status straight from Shopify, it does not simulate or guess it.

Billing, staff accounts & roles, security/2FA, payment gateway setup, and domain management are Shopify account-level controls. Shopify does not expose write access to these outside its own console for any app, for account-security reasons — so they open Shopify's real settings directly rather than being faked here.
